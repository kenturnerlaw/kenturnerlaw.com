Don't Pay Your Traffic Ticket Yet!

Paying your traffic ticket is an admission of guilt and acceptance of the fine when you may be able to have the ticket dismissed, the fine reduced, and protect your driver's license.

When you pay the ticket, you give up the opportunity to pursue those options.

Most people only look at the amount of the fine and think that hiring an attorney is not worth the cost. The real cost is often much higher. A traffic conviction can increase your insurance premiums, add points to your driving record, affect your driver's license, and cost you far more than the ticket itself.

In many cases, hiring an attorney costs less than the ticket itself and will almost certainly cost you less than the long-term cost of simply paying the ticket.

Even if you believe the ticket was justified, paying it is probably not your best option. Before you admit guilt and accept the fine, find out what options may be available to reduce the consequences, protect your driver's license, and avoid unnecessary long-term costs.

Why Hire an Attorney?

Most people think they have only two choices—pay the ticket or take a day off work and go to court themselves. In reality, there is usually another option.

In the vast majority of traffic cases, you will not have to appear in court. We can appear on your behalf, saving you the inconvenience of taking time off work, losing wages, driving to the courthouse, finding parking, waiting for your case to be called, and wondering what to say to the judge.

Many people are surprised to learn that the attorney's fee is often less than the increase in insurance premiums alone, not to mention the value of your time, lost wages, and the potential impact on your driving record.

An Attorney May Be Able To:

  • Obtain a dismissal.
  • Reduce the fine.
  • Help you avoid points when legally available.
  • Protect your driver's license.
  • Negotiate a better outcome than simply paying the ticket.
  • Appear in court so you don't have to.
  • Save you from taking time off work.
  • Help you avoid the long-term consequences of a traffic conviction.

While no attorney can promise a particular outcome, our goal is always the same—to obtain the best result possible while saving you time, money, and unnecessary consequences.
