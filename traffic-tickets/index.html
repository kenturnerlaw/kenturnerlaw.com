Do Not Pay Your Traffic Ticket Yet!

Paying the ticket may be the fastest way to get it off your desk. It may also be the most expensive choice once points, insurance, your driving record, and future consequences are considered.

Even if you believe the ticket was justified, paying it is probably not your best option before you know what can be done.

A Traffic Ticket Is a Decision, Not Just a Fine

When you pay a Florida traffic ticket, you are not simply paying money to make the problem disappear. You are making a choice about how the case will be resolved.

That choice can affect points on your license, whether adjudication is entered, insurance costs, traffic-school eligibility, and what happens if you receive another citation later.

In many cases, hiring an attorney costs less than the long-term consequences of simply paying the ticket. You may also be able to avoid missing work or appearing in court yourself.
