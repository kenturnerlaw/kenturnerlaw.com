Traffic Ticket Attorney

Got a traffic ticket? Do not just pay it before you know your options.

Paying a ticket can mean points, traffic school, higher insurance costs, and giving up the opportunity to have the charge reduced or dismissed. Even if you believe the ticket was justified, paying it is probably not your best option until you know what can be done.

Before You Pay the Ticket

Paying the fine closes off options. Depending on the ticket and your driving record, an attorney may be able to seek dismissal, a reduction, withheld adjudication, fewer points, or another result that protects your license and limits the long-term cost of the citation.
