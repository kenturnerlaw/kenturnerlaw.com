What this calculator includes

It applies the guideline schedule in Florida Statutes § 61.30, each parent's income percentage, qualifying child-care and health-related expenses, credits for the parent who prepays those expenses, and the substantial time-sharing formula when each parent has at least 20% of the overnights.

What it does not decide

The calculator estimates 2026 federal income and payroll taxes from the information entered. It does not determine imputed income beyond its minimum-wage floor, unusual income, tax adjustments or credits other than the child tax credit, low-income limits, retroactive support, arrears, deviations, Social Security benefit credits, split custody, or more than six children.

Guideline source checked August 13, 2026: Florida Statutes § 61.30.
