What the tax estimate includes

The calculator uses the 2026 federal income-tax brackets and standard deduction for the filing status selected, an estimated Child Tax Credit based on the qualifying-child count entered, 2026 Social Security tax up to the taxable wage base, Medicare tax, and the self-employment tax rates when self-employed income is selected.

What it does not decide

It does not determine every tax deduction or credit, additional Medicare tax, earned-income credits, special business deductions, imputed income beyond its public minimum-wage floor, unusual income, low-income limits, retroactive support, arrears, deviations, Social Security benefit credits, split custody, or more than six children.

Guideline source checked August 13, 2026: Florida Statutes § 61.30.
