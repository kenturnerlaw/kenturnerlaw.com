Florida limited-scope representation

Unbundled Legal Services in Florida

Affordable legal help when you do not need full representation. You choose the services. You control the cost. Use attorney time only when you need it.

Pay Only for the Legal Help You Need

Many legal matters do not require hiring an attorney to handle every aspect of your case. With unbundled legal services, also called limited scope representation, you decide how much legal assistance you need and pay only for those services.

Whether you need help reviewing documents, preparing court forms, developing a strategy, or appearing at a single hearing, you can retain an attorney for just that portion of your case.

Flexible, Affordable Pricing

Most unbundled legal services are available on a flat-fee basis, with fees typically ranging from $100 to $750, depending on the complexity and time required.

For work that does not fit a flat-fee structure, services are available at $300 per hour, with a one-hour minimum.

Sliding-scale fees may be available in appropriate cases based on financial need and the nature of the legal matter.

Rather than paying a large retainer for full representation, you can use attorney time only when you need it. Purchase an hour for legal advice today, return later for document review, or schedule assistance before an important hearing.

Common Unbundled Legal Services

  • Legal advice and case strategy
  • Document review and editing
  • Drafting motions, petitions, and responses
  • Financial affidavit preparation and review
  • Settlement agreement review
  • Mediation preparation
  • Trial and hearing preparation
  • Representation at a single hearing, mediation, or case management conference
  • Legal research and case evaluation

How Limited Scope Representation Works

Florida permits limited scope representation under Rule 4-1.2(c) of the Rules Regulating The Florida Bar. Before work begins, you and your attorney sign a written agreement clearly defining the services to be provided.

You remain responsible for any portions of your case outside the agreed scope, including deadlines, filings, and court appearances unless the written agreement says otherwise.

Is This Right for You?

Unbundled legal services may be a good option if you need professional guidance without the cost of full representation, want an attorney to review your work, need help preparing for mediation or a hearing, want representation at one specific proceeding, or prefer to control your legal costs.
